## C. VIEC PHAI QUYET TRUOC KHI THEM

### C1. Danh so cho 25 ban ghi moi - QUYET NGAY BAY GIO

So hien chay lien tuc theo tung chuong:

    C1: 001-021   C2: 022-028   C3: 029-035   C4: 036-051
    C5: 052-067   C6: 068-093   C7: 094-111   C8: 112-126

Them ban ghi moi vao giua thi vo the lien tuc do. Hai cach:

**Cach 1 - noi so moi vao cuoi day (127, 128, ...)**
Khong dung gi den so cu. Nhuoc diem: ban ghi cua chuong 3 lai mang so 127,
nhin vao ma khong doan duoc chuong.

**Cach 2 - danh lai so cho C2 den C8 (KHUYEN NGHI)**
Giu nguyen C1 (dang co mapping + 46 ham Python tro vao). Danh lai tu C2:
moi chuong lai lien tuc, cac ban ghi moi nam dung cho.

**Day la co hoi cuoi cung de danh lai re.** Hien C2-C8 chua co mapping va chua
co ham Python nao tro vao, nen danh lai khong pha gi. Khi da viet ham Python
roi thi danh lai se lam mo coi toan bo - dung nguyen tac "khong bao gio danh
lai so" ma chinh co Lan da dat ra.

### C2. L10_C7_B19_VD104A

Noi dung khac VD104 nen la mot don vi kien thuc rieng, phai co so rieng chu
khong phai hau to A (xem docs/04_ID_STANDARD.md). Neu chon Cach 2 thi giai
quyet luon trong lan danh lai.

Them nua: `scope` cua VD104A dang bi chep nham tu VD104 ("duong tron di qua ba
diem") trong khi noi dung la "dieu kien khong truc tiep cho truoc".

### C3. Co ghi nguon khong?

Mot so ban ghi khong co trong van ban Bo ma lay tu SGK Ket noi tri thuc:
NB001, NB007, NB008, NB015, TH021/VD021 (chuong 1); TH072, VD079 (chuong 6);
NB117-119 (chuong 8); va cac ban ghi "nhan biet khai niem" khac.

Deu hop li va deu can de ra de. Nhung neu hoi dong soi "cau nay can cu vao
dau", co mot truong `nguon` voi hai gia tri `CT2018` / `SGK_KNTT` thi tra loi
duoc ngay. Claude da de xuat, co Lan chua chot.

## Ba cho co y KHONG sua

**B3 - quy tac cong/nhan dang o muc TH trong khi Bo dung dong tu "Van dung
duoc".** Neu ha het xuong hay nang het len thi bai 21 mat can mot muc. Da giu
nguyen cac ban ghi TH va THEM mot ban ghi VD rieng, de bai 21 co du ca hai muc.

**B4 - TH122/VD122 dung verb "Thiet lap duoc dieu kien" trong khi Bo viet
"Nhan biet duoc hai duong thang cat nhau, song song...".** Giu nguyen vi neu
theo dung chu cua Bo thi phai ha xuong muc NB, ma xet vi tri tuong doi cua hai
duong thang bang toa do la viec phai tinh toan, khong phai nhan biet. Sua theo
chu se lam ngan hang de kem di.

**B7, B8 - VD155 (he so trong khai trien) va VD090 (gia tri ngoai le, bieu do
hop) khong co trong chuong trinh cot loi.** VD155 thuoc Chuyen de 10.1, VD090
thuoc SGK Ket noi tri thuc. Giu lai vi co Lan day lop chuyen Toan va ca hai
deu la dang ra de that. Neu ra de dai tra thi khong chon hai ma nay.

## Ghi nho cho lan sau

Truong `nguon` (CT2018 / SGK_KNTT) van chua duoc chot. Neu sau nay hoi dong
hoi "cau nay can cu vao dau" thi cac ban ghi lay tu SGK se kho tra loi ngay.
